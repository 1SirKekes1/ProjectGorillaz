## The Cursed Forest
> Navigate through the cursed forest to find the ancient artifact.
cover: /images/forest.jpg

=== Step ForestEntrance
* You enter the forest. The path splits into three. Which way do you go?
image: /images/forest.jpg
- Take the left path -> River
- Take the middle path -> Altar
- Take the right path -> LostInForest

=== Step River
* You encounter a river. Do you swim across or look for a bridge?
image: /images/forest.jpg
- Swim across -> FoundArtifact
- Look for a bridge -> FoundArtifact

=== Step Altar
* You find a mysterious altar. Do you interact with it?
image: /images/forest.jpg
- Interact with the altar -> FoundArtifact
- Ignore it and continue -> LostInForest

=== Step LostInForest (LOSE)
* You were lost in the forest
image: /images/forest.jpg

=== Step FoundArtifact (WIN)
* You found the artifact
image: /images/forest.jpg
