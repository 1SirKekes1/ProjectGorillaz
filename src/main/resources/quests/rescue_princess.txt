Quest: Rescue the Princess
* Save the princess from the dragon's lair.
image: dragon.jpg

Step: LairEntrance
* You arrive at the dragon's lair. Do you enter?
image: dragon.jpg
- Enter the lair -> DragonSleeping
- Look for another way -> SecretPassage

Step: DragonSleeping
* You see the dragon sleeping. Do you attack or sneak past?
image: dragon.jpg
- Attack the dragon -> DefeatedDragon
- Sneak past -> CaughtByDragon

Step: SecretPassage
* You find a secret passage. Do you take it?
image: dragon.jpg
- Take the passage -> DefeatedDragon
- Return to the entrance -> LairEntrance

Step: DefeatedDragon (WIN)
* You defeated the dragon and saved the princess
image: dragon.jpg

Step: CaughtByDragon (LOSE)
* You failed and the dragon caught you
