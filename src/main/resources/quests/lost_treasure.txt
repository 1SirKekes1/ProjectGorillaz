## The Lost Treasure
> Find the hidden treasure in the ancient ruins.
cover: /images/treasure.jpg

=== Step Start
* You are standing in front of the ruins. There are two paths: left and right.
image: /images/number1.jpg
- Go left -> Cave
- Go right -> Cave

=== Step Cave
* You find a dark cave. Do you enter?
image: /images/number2.jpg
- Enter the cave -> ShinyObject
- Go back -> Start

=== Step ShinyObject
* You see a shiny object in the distance. Do you approach it?
image: /images/number3.jpg
- Approach the object -> FoundTreasure
- Ignore it and continue -> Lost

=== Step FoundTreasure (WIN)
* You found diamonds
image: /images/treasure.jpg

=== Step Lost (LOSE)
* You lost
image: /images/number3.jpg
